These are my new commander decks that I love to play. They are very different decks: Kefka grinds opponents' hands to nothing, Y'shtola drains the table one expensive spell at a time, and Avatar Aang ramps into a commander that rebuilds itself every turn. Two of them are control decks and Avatar Aang is a midrange pile that plays like a puzzle to get him to flip to the backside. Let's get into it!

| Deck | Colors | Archetype | Primary win | Plays best when |
|------|--------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| [Kefka, Court Mage](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/322/kefka-court-mage-kefka-ruler-of-ruin) | Grixis ({U}{B}{R}) | Symmetrical discard, punisher, reanimator | Discard drain ([Waste Not](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/117/waste-not), [Monument to Endurance](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/237/monument-to-endurance)) + [Kefka, Ruler of Ruin](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/322/kefka-court-mage-kefka-ruler-of-ruin) card advantage | Opponents rely on cards in hand |
| [Y'shtola, Night's Blessed](https://scryfall.com/card/fic/7/yshtola-nights-blessed) | Esper ({W}{U}{B}) | Spellslinger control / drain | Repeated 2-point pings from MV 3+ spells, backed by [Sheoldred](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/107/sheoldred-the-apocalypse) and [Propaganda](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/151/propaganda)-style stax | The table is slow enough for interaction to matter |
| [Avatar Aang](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/207/avatar-aang-aang-master-of-elements) | Five-color identity ({R}{G}{W}{U} cast, {B} in back face) | Avatar TLA Theme deck | Flipping to [Aang, Master of Elements](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/207/avatar-aang-aang-master-of-elements) for 4 damage, 4 cards, 4 life each upkeep | You can trigger all four bends in one turn |

## Kefka, Court Mage (Grixis)

[Kefka](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/322/kefka-court-mage-kefka-ruler-of-ruin) turns every ETB and attack into a table-wide discard that refills your hand, then punishes opponents for every card they lose. The deck wins by grinding hands empty while discard triggers drain life, and flips Kefka late to convert that life loss into cards.

### The commander

- **Front** ({2}{U}{B}{R}, 4/5 Wizard): when [Kefka, Court Mage](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/322/kefka-court-mage-kefka-ruler-of-ruin) enters or attacks, each player discards a card, then you draw one card per card type among everything discarded. A land, a creature and an instant in the pile draws you three.
- **{8}, sorcery speed**: each opponent sacrifices a permanent, and Kefka transforms.
- **Back, [Kefka, Ruler of Ruin](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/322/kefka-court-mage-kefka-ruler-of-ruin)** (flying): whenever an opponent loses life during your turn, you draw that many cards. Every drain on your turn becomes card advantage.

### How the deck works

1. **Multiply Kefka's triggers.** [Harmonic Prodigy](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/123/harmonic-prodigy) and [Roaming Throne](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/258/roaming-throne) (naming Wizard, sometimes naming Human does that trick too for [Doctor Doom](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/6/doctor-doom-king-of-latveria)) double each ETB and attack trigger. [Saw in Half](https://scryfall.com/card/unf/88/saw-in-half) makes two Kefkas and two ETBs, but only keeps one (hint: pick the *real* one).
2. **Re-trigger the ETB.** [Thassa, Deep-Dwelling](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/71/thassa-deep-dwelling) blinks him every end step, [Displacer Kitten](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/63/displacer-kitten) does it on each noncreature spell, and [Riptide Laboratory](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/303/riptide-laboratory) returns him to hand as a Wizard. Blinking a flipped Kefka brings back the front face, so choose which side you need. I typically like to save this effect when removal is pointed at [Kefka](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/322/kefka-court-mage-kefka-ruler-of-ruin).
3. **Punish every discard.** [Waste Not](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/117/waste-not), [Monument to Endurance](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/237/monument-to-endurance), [Tinybones, Trinket Thief](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/17/tinybones-trinket-thief), [Bone Miser](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/15/bone-miser) and [Geth's Grimoire](https://scryfall.com/card/dst/123/geths-grimoire) turn each forced discard into mana, cards, zombies or drain. [Liliana's Caress](https://scryfall.com/card/m11/103/lilianas-caress), [Painful Quandary](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/179/painful-quandary), [Raven's Crime](https://scryfall.com/card/mma/95/ravens-crime) and [Syphon Mind](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/159/syphon-mind) stack more discards on top.
4. **Profit from your own discards.** [Faithless Looting](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/244/faithless-looting), [Frantic Search](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/159/frantic-search), [Attunement](https://scryfall.com/card/usg/61/attunement) and the cycling lands dump fuel. [Kroxa, Titan of Death's Hunger](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/221/kroxa-titan-of-deaths-hunger) escapes, and [Animate Dead](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/207/animate-dead), [Reanimate](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/155/reanimate), and [Afterlife from the Loam](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/25/afterlife-from-the-loam).
5. **Close with drains.** [Bloodchief Ascension](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/139/bloodchief-ascension) and the [Monument to Endurance](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/237/monument-to-endurance) pings add up quickly once hands are empty. With [Ruler of Ruin](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/322/kefka-court-mage-kefka-ruler-of-ruin) out, each point lost on your turn draws a card. A more brutal way is to get [Painful Quandary](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/179/painful-quandary) or [Quest for the Nihil Stone](https://scryfall.com/card/wwk/64/quest-for-the-nihil-stone) active.
6. **Protection for Kefka.** [Malakir Rebirth](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/111/malakir-rebirth-malakir-mire), [Hide on the Ceiling](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/249/hide-on-the-ceiling), and [Essence Flux](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/354/essence-flux) all provide protection, while making opponents discard again.

### Piloting notes

- Kefka's draw counts card types across everyone's discards, so hold a varied hand yourself to guarantee two or three types.
- The {8} flip is a late-game mana sink. Ramp hard early; the Signets and Talismans exist to reach it. I do feel that the front side achieves a lot of what the deck wants to do. This back side is just gravy.
- Empty hands make [Storm World](https://scryfall.com/card/me3/111/storm-world) and [Quest for the Nihil Stone](https://scryfall.com/card/wwk/64/quest-for-the-nihil-stone) active. Time them after two or three Kefka triggers.
- [Bojuka Bog](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/363/bojuka-bog) exists to stamp out any graveyard matters cards from the opponent.

## Y'shtola, Night's Blessed (Esper)

[Y'shtola](https://scryfall.com/card/fic/7/yshtola-nights-blessed) is an interaction-heavy control deck where every answer is also a burn spell. Each noncreature spell with mana value 3+ pings the table for 2 and gains 2, and big turns of life loss draw extra cards at end step. Mixed with other effects that do the same, once you have control with Y'shtola, it is just an inevitability to win.

### The commander

- **{1}{W}{U}{B}, 2/4 Cat Warlock, vigilance.**
- **Drain**: whenever you cast a noncreature spell with mana value 3 or greater, she deals 2 damage to each opponent and you gain 2 life.
- **Draw**: at the beginning of each end step, if any player lost 4+ life that turn, you draw a card. It checks every end step, not just yours.

### How the deck works

1. **Mana value, not mana paid.** The trigger reads the card's mana value, so free and cheap-to-cast spells still count. [Fierce Guardianship](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/94/fierce-guardianship), [Deadly Rollick](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/147/deadly-rollick), [Snuff Out](https://scryfall.com/card/gvl/53/snuff-out), [Misdirection](https://scryfall.com/card/ddt/15/misdirection), [Flawless Maneuver](https://scryfall.com/card/frc/25/flawless-maneuver) and [Sickening Shoal](https://scryfall.com/card/bok/82/sickening-shoal) all trigger Y'shtola for zero mana. [Rewind](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/63/rewind) untaps four lands after triggering her.
2. **Reduce costs, keep the trigger.** [Jet Medallion](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/292/jet-medallion) and [Sapphire Medallion](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/296/sapphire-medallion), [Archmage of Runes](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/30/archmage-of-runes) and [Lyse Hext](https://scryfall.com/card/fic/88/lyse-hext) lower what you pay while mana value stays the same.
3. **Double and extend the drain.** [Delney](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/12/delney-streetwise-lookout) doubles triggers from creatures with power 2 or less, and Y'shtola has power 2. [Enduring Tenacity](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/95/enduring-tenacity) turns each 2-life gain into 2 more loss for one opponent, which also clears the 4-life draw threshold on its own. [Sheoldred, the Apocalypse](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/107/sheoldred-the-apocalypse) and [Kambal](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/237/kambal-consul-of-allocation) add passive drain.
4. **Draw off the triggers.** [Curiosity](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/147/curiosity), [Archmage Emeritus](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/188/archmage-emeritus), [Sygg](https://scryfall.com/card/znc/103/sygg-river-cutthroat) and [Talion](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/215/talion-the-kindly-lord) refill the hand, so you never run out of spells to cast.
5. **Answer everything.** The deck carries roughly 20 pieces of removal and counters, and most of them trigger Y'shtola.

### Piloting notes

- Hold free spells for opponents' turns. The 2 damage lands on their turn and can trigger the end-step draw for you. It helps when an opponent attacks another opponent and you can get pile up the damage to get the end-of-turn draw.
- Otherwise, two qualifying spells in one turn equals 4 damage to every opponent, which draws you a card.
- Put [Curiosity](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/147/curiosity) or [Helm of the Ghastlord](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/166/helm-of-the-ghastlord) on Y'shtola: each trigger damages every opponent, so it draws once per opponent.
- [Waterlogged Teachings](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/261/waterlogged-teachings-inundated-archive), [Mystic Sanctuary](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/388/mystic-sanctuary) and [Otawara](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/271/otawara-soaring-city) provide late-game utility.

## Avatar Aang (Five-Color)

[Avatar Aang](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/207/avatar-aang-aang-master-of-elements) is a ramp-and-value deck built around mastering all four bending actions in one turn. Each time he flips, Aang can later flip back at an upkeep for 4 damage to each opponent, 4 cards, 4 life and four +1/+1 counters, then re-flip with four more bends. This is the main point of this deck to try and flip Aang. There are a marginal amount of creature tutors to fetch the ally you need to complete the cycle.

### The commander

- **Front** ({R}{G}{W}{U}, 4/4 flying, firebending 2): whenever you waterbend, earthbend, firebend or airbend, draw a card. If you've done all four this turn, he transforms. The check covers the whole turn, including bends made before he entered.
- **Back, [Aang, Master of Elements](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/207/avatar-aang-aang-master-of-elements)** (6/6 flying): your spells cost {W}{U}{B}{R}{G} less, which also reduces generic costs. At the beginning of each upkeep you may transform him back; if you do, you gain 4 life, draw 4, put four +1/+1 counters on him and deal 4 damage to each opponent.
- **Color identity is all five colors**, because the back face's rules text carries the {W}, {U}, {B}, {R} and {G} symbols. That is why [Putrefy](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/131/putrefy), [Anguished Unmaking](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/293/anguished-unmaking) and [Toxic Deluge](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/161/toxic-deluge) are legal here.

### The four bends

| Bend | What it does | Main sources in the deck |
|------|-------------|--------------------------|
| Waterbend | Tap artifacts or creatures to help pay a cost | [Katara](https://scryfall.com/card/jtla/12/katara) (3 versions), [Yue](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/83/yue-the-moon-spirit), [Waterbender Ascension](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/79/waterbender-ascension), [Waterbender's Restoration](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/99/waterbenders-restoration), [Spirit Water Revival](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/73/spirit-water-revival) |
| Earthbend | A land becomes a hasty creature with +1/+1 counters, and returns if it dies | [Toph](https://scryfall.com/card/jtla/45/toph) (4 versions), [Haru](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/182/haru-hidden-talent), [Bumi](https://scryfall.com/card/jtla/38/bumi), [Badgermole Cub](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/167/badgermole-cub), [Earthbender Ascension](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/175/earthbender-ascension), [Earthshape](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/67/earthshape) |
| Firebend | Adds red mana when the creature attacks | [Avatar Aang](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/207/avatar-aang-aang-master-of-elements) (firebending 2), and [Iroh, Grand Lotus](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/227/iroh-grand-lotus) |
| Airbend | Exile a creature or spell; its owner may recast it for {2} | Aang (several versions), Appa (2), [Airbending Lesson](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/8/airbending-lesson), [Airbender's Reversal](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/7/airbenders-reversal), [Airbender Ascension](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/6/airbender-ascension) |

### How the deck works

1. **Ramp early.** [Farseek](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/173/farseek), [Nature's Lore](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/177/natures-lore), [Three Visits](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/181/three-visits), [Delighted Halfling](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/158/delighted-halfling), [Bloom Tender](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/166/bloom-tender), [Harabaz Druid](https://scryfall.com/card/wwk/105/harabaz-druid), [Arcane Signet](https://scryfall.com/card/frc/20/arcane-signet) and [Chromatic Lantern](https://scryfall.com/card/mbc/73/chromatic-lantern) get you to Aang by turn 3–4.
2. **Assemble three bends.** Cast your waterbend, earthbend and airbend pieces precombat, drawing a card off each with Aang out.
3. **Attack with Aang for the fourth.** His own firebending 2 triggers on attack, completing the set and flipping him mid-combat.
4. **Flip back each upkeep.** Flipping back at an upkeep deals 4 to each opponent and draws 4. Doing it on your own upkeep gives you a full hand to re-assemble the four bends that same turn, so the loop fires once per turn cycle.
5. **Break the board.** With {W}{U}{B}{R}{G} off every spell, most of your hand becomes free, so you can dump the rest and redo the bends.

### Piloting notes

- Fire is the thinnest element. Aang's attack is the most reliable firebend, so keep him alive and able to swing: [Swiftfoot Boots](https://scryfall.com/card/mbc/77/swiftfoot-boots), [Heroic Intervention](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/295/heroic-intervention) and [Enter the Avatar State](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/18/enter-the-avatar-state) are there for that.
- Flipping back is optional. Staying on [Master of Elements](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/207/avatar-aang-aang-master-of-elements) keeps the {W}{U}{B}{R}{G} discount; flip back when the 4 damage and 4 cards beat the discount, or when you can re-bend right away.
- Airbend works on your own things too: exile your own creature to reset its ETB and recast it for {2}.
